# 📊 Learning Statistics & Memory Analytics

The **Learning Statistics** screen provides comprehensive, real-time insights into your memory retention, study habits, cognitive workload forecasts, and vocabulary growth.

![The Statistics screen, showing retention rate, the review-activity heatmap, and the 7-day forecast](learning-statistics)

---

## 📍 How to Open the Statistics Screen

You can access the Statistics screen from two convenient places:
1. **From the Home Dashboard**: Tap the **Retention Ring Widget** (the percentage circle on the top right of the Daily Load card).
2. **From the Decks Tab**: Tap the **Stats ↗** button inside the **Study Progress** banner at the top of the Decks screen.

---

## 🔍 Detailed Component Breakdown

### 1. Core Memory Metrics
- **30-Day Retention Rate**: The percentage of flashcards successfully recalled during reviews over the last 30 days. Gives you a direct measurement of memory stability.
- **Active Study Streak**: Number of consecutive days you have completed your scheduled daily reviews.
- **Total Saved Cards**: The total volume of active vocabulary across all your decks.
- **New This Week**: Count of brand-new words introduced and studied over the past 7 days.

---

### 2. Review Activity Heatmap
- **5-Week Activity Grid**: A visual calendar matrix (similar to GitHub's contribution chart).
- **Color Intensity**: Darker squares represent days with higher review volume, allowing you to easily spot study patterns and build long-term consistency.

---

### 3. 7-Day Review Forecast
- **FSRS Cognitive Prediction**: Leverages the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler algorithm to project exactly how many cards will become due on each of the upcoming 7 days.
- **Workload Planning**: Helps you anticipate upcoming heavy study days (e.g. after adding many new words) and plan your study time accordingly.

---

### 4. Vocabulary Growth Trajectory
- A historical timeline chart displaying your cumulative vocabulary acquisition across the past 7 weeks.
- Visualizes steady progress and motivates continuous learning.

---

### 5. Difficult Words (Top 10 Lapsed Cards)
- **Automatic "Leech" Detection**: Identifies the 10 cards in your library with the highest number of memory lapses (cards frequently rated as "Again").
- **Lapse Counter**: Shows how many times you missed each word.
- **One-Tap Practice Button**: Tap the practice arrow next to any difficult word to jump straight into a focused study drill for that card, helping you overcome memory blocks without waiting for its next scheduled interval.

---

## 💡 Common Workflows

1. **Planning Your Weekly Study Load**:
   - Open the Statistics screen $\rightarrow$ check the **7-Day Review Forecast**.
   - If Wednesday shows a high volume of due cards, you can do extra reviews on Tuesday or avoid introducing new cards on that day.
2. **Targeted Weak-Word Drilling**:
   - Scroll down to **Difficult Words**.
   - Tap **Practice** next to your most failed word to review its context sentence, meaning, and audio immediately.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

> [!TIP]
> **What is a healthy 30-day retention rate?**
> In spaced repetition systems, a retention rate between **85% and 90%** is optimal. Higher retention (e.g. 98%) often means you are over-reviewing, while lower retention (< 80%) indicates too many new cards or intervals that are too aggressive.

> [!NOTE]
> **Why are difficult words highlighted separately?**
> Cognitive science shows that drilling challenging cards outside of their regular schedule helps strengthen weak neural pathways and prevents "leech" frustration.
