# Lemmory: Stop Memorizing. Start Speaking.

## 😩 The Hook

Be honest: how many German or Spanish words have you "learned" and then completely forgotten by next Tuesday?

You download a language app. You grind through the same five flashcards for the hundredth time. You memorize "der Hund" means dog — but the second a real German sentence throws it at you in a different form, you're lost. No context. No grammar explanation. No idea *why* it's "den Hund" this time and not "der Hund." So you close the app, again, and tell yourself you'll "get back to it."

Here's the real problem: most language apps teach you to recognize flashcards, not a language. They dump word lists on you with zero context, recycle the same tired sentences, and treat every learner like they're starting from zero — even if you already know 2,000 words. Meanwhile, the *actual* words you want to learn — the ones from that show you're watching, that podcast, that conversation with a coworker — never even make it into your deck.

You don't need more flashcards. You need a system that actually gets smarter the more you use it.

## ⚡ The Solution

Meet **Lemmory** — the AI-native vocabulary app that turns any word, from any source, into a real, lasting memory.

Here's the core idea: instead of static flashcards someone else wrote in 2015, Lemmory generates a complete, personalized lesson for *every single word* — instantly. Meanings, real example sentences, synonyms, common phrases, even grammar explained in plain English. Then it hands that word off to a genuinely smart spaced-repetition engine (the same science serious learners already trust) that decides exactly when you need to see it again — not too soon, not too late, just right.

Lemmory isn't another app fighting for five minutes of your attention. It's the tool that turns "I sort of remember that word" into "I just used that word correctly without thinking."

## 🚀 How It Works

**1. Type any word, get a full lesson.**
Search any German or Spanish word — even ones you've never seen. Lemmory generates meaning, usage, and examples in seconds. No pre-built dictionary limits. If it's a real word, Lemmory's got it.

**2. Smart flashcards that adapt to you.**
Swipe, rate how well you knew it, done. Lemmory's scheduling engine tracks your real memory and resurfaces words exactly when you're about to forget them — not on some rigid, one-size-fits-all timer.

**3. Capture words from real life.**
Heard a word on Netflix? Read one in an article? Drop it in your Mining Queue and turn it into a full card later, in one tap. Your vocabulary grows from things you actually care about.

**4. Practice it every way that matters.**
Standard flashcards, fill-in-the-blank cloze cards, and reverse mode (English → target language). Recognizing a word and *producing* it are different skills — Lemmory trains both.

**5. Ask AI when you're confused.**
Don't get Konjunktiv II? Wondering how "sprechen" differs from "reden"? Just ask. Get a real, plain-English answer, right inside the card.

**6. No forced subscription.**
Bring your own AI key (OpenAI, Gemini, and more) and pay only for what you actually use — or skip keys entirely with Lemmory's free offline dictionary. Your call.

**7. Already using Anki?**
Import your existing decks — review history included. You've put in the work already; Lemmory doesn't make you start over.

**8. Your words, everywhere, forever.**
Sync across your phone and tablet. Export your full library anytime. Your data is never trapped, and your API keys never leave your device.
