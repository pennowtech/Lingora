1. Audio & Linguistic Senses

Tap the speaker icon (🔊) to stream crisp neural native pronunciation. For words with multiple meanings (e.g. Zug = Train ➔ Draft ➔ Move), swipe horizontally to switch senses.

Sub-sense CEFR level tracking

Each individual sense maintains its own independent CEFR difficulty tag (A1 through C2). Swipe Left ➔ Again, Swipe Right ➔ Good.

🖼️ Screenshot: Multi-Sense Switcher Tap to Expand 🔍
💡 Pro-Tip: Grammar Targeting
You can specify subordinate clauses (weil/dass) or subjunctive tenses (Konjunktiv II) to generate custom flashcards!
