Privacy Policy & Account Data Deletion

What Lemony stores

Lemony is offline-first: your vocabulary decks, cards, and review history live on your device. AI provider API keys you enter (OpenAI, Mistral, Gemini, Anthropic, DeepL) are stored only in your device's secure keychain and are never sent anywhere except directly to the provider you configured.

If you choose to sign in with Google to sync your decks and review progress across devices, that data (decks, cards, review history — never your API keys) is stored in a Google Firestore database, scoped to your account and readable only by you.

Deleting your account & synced data

You can permanently delete everything Lemony has synced to the cloud in either of two ways:

1. In the app

  1. Open Lemony and go to Settings → Sync.
  2. Sign in with Google if you aren't already.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account & sync data.
  4. Confirm. This immediately erases every synced record from the cloud and signs you out. Your local decks and cards on this device are unaffected.

2. From the web, without the app

What happens after a request

Whichever method you use, all documents under your account's synced data are deleted from Firestore outright (not just hidden), your Google sign-in session is revoked on this device, and no further sync can happen until you sign in again — which starts a new, empty synced state.
